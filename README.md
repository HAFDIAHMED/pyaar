# PYAAR — The Embrace

> The Beloved at the centre, surrounded by friends. The Lover cannot be captured.
> Bring her to the Heart with two companions around her — that is love, that is the win.

PYAAR is an original 1-vs-1 strategy game in the chess family, built around a mechanic that doesn't exist anywhere else: **the goal piece cannot be captured.** The Lover walks slowly toward the centre of the board; the opponent cannot remove her, only block her, trap her, or starve her of companions. You win by making one picture — Lover on the Heart, two of your other pieces in the surrounding Lotus. Or by besieging the enemy army until their Lover stands alone.

**Tested across 200+ AI-vs-AI games:** 80% Embrace endings, 6% Siege, ~50/50 first-mover balance, ~30 moves per game.

## How to play

1. Double-click **`index.html`** — it opens in your browser.
2. **Arrange your back row** in any order you like (click two of your pieces to swap; Begin Game when done).
3. Click your piece, click a glowing cell. The computer (Player 2) takes its turn automatically.
4. Read **`MANUAL.html`** for the full design and strategy. **`RULES.md`** is the one-page summary.

## What's different from chess

| Chess | PYAAR |
|---|---|
| Capture the goal piece (King) to win | The goal piece (**Lover**) cannot be captured |
| Win = one cell where opponent's King moves | Win = a **picture**: your Lover at the centre with two pieces around her |
| Fixed starting position | **You arrange your back row** before each game |
| 64 squares, 16 pieces per side | 49 squares, 8 pieces per side |
| 60+ minute games | 5–15 minute games |

## The eight pieces (per side)

♥ **Lover** (the crown-piece — uncapturable) · ⚔ **Warrior** (king move) · ✦ **Sage** (bishop) · ⚯ **Traitor** (rook) · ◊ **Fool** (knight) · ⚜ **Guardian** (short queen) · ☾ **Dreamer** (3-cell pacifist scout) · ⚑ **Soldier** (pawn).
