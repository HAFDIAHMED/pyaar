# PYAAR

### *The Embrace*

> Bring her to the centre, surrounded by friends.
> She is the Beloved, and she cannot be touched.

PYAAR is a 1-vs-1 strategy game on a **7 × 7 board**. Each player commands **8 pieces**: seven combat characters and one **Lover** — the Beloved, who is the only piece that can win the game. There is exactly **one way to win**, and it is a picture: the Lover at the Heart of the board, surrounded by two of her companions.

---

## The board

A 7 × 7 checkerboard.

- **Row 1** — Player 1's seven combat characters.
- **Row 2, column 4** — Player 1's Soldier.
- **Row 6, column 4** — Player 2's Soldier.
- **Row 7** — Player 2's seven combat characters.
- **Cell (4, 4)** — **The Heart**.
- **The 8 cells around the Heart** — the **Lotus**. This is where the Embrace forms.

---

## Setup — before the first move

Each player arranges their **seven combat pieces** (Lover + 6 others) along their home row in any order they like. Click two of your back-row pieces to swap them; **Shuffle Mine** randomises; **Begin Game** confirms.

- The **Soldier** is *not* arrangeable — he always starts at (2, 4) / (6, 4).
- Player 1 arranges first. Player 2's row shuffles automatically in vs Computer, or is arranged by hand in Hot-seat.

---

## The eight pieces per side

| Piece | Movement | Captures |
|---|---|---|
| **♥ Lover** | 1 cell — only toward the Heart. | Yes, by displacement. **Cannot be captured by enemies.** |
| **⚔ Warrior** | 1 cell in any of 8 directions. | Yes. *(king)* |
| **✦ Sage** | Any cells diagonally — stops at the first piece. | First enemy in path. *(bishop)* |
| **⚯ Traitor** | Any cells orthogonally — stops at the first piece. | First enemy in path. *(rook)* |
| **◊ Fool** | L-shape (2 + 1), jumps over anything. | Yes, by landing. *(knight)* |
| **⚜ Guardian** | Up to 2 cells in a straight line, any direction. | Yes, by landing. |
| **☾ Dreamer** | Up to 3 cells in a straight line, any direction. | **No — cannot capture.** Must end on an empty cell. |
| **⚑ Soldier** | 1 cell straight forward. | 1 cell diagonally forward (pawn-style). No retreat. |

**The Lover is sacred.** No enemy piece may capture her or move onto her cell. The opponent must work around her; they may block her or trap her, but they may never remove her.

---

## How to win — two paths

### ❀ The Embrace (the main path)
At the **start of your turn**, both must be true:
1. Your **Lover** stands on the **Heart** (4, 4).
2. **At least 2 of your other pieces** stand on the 8 cells of the Lotus around her.

That is the win. *The Beloved at the centre, ringed by her companions.* The "start of your turn" requirement means the opponent had one full move to disrupt; you held the formation through one storm.

### ⚔ The Siege (the secondary path)
At the **start of your opponent's turn**, they have **no legal move**. By the nature of PYAAR this almost always means their combat pieces are gone and their Lover stands alone, trapped. The army opposite has won the siege.

---

## How to lose

- The opponent achieves The Embrace or The Siege before you do.
- If after 100 moves neither has formed an Embrace, the **closer-to-Heart** army wins by **Closer to the Heart** (tiebreaker by Lover distance, then by escort count).

---

## Why the Lover is uncapturable

In every other piece-and-board game in this family (chess, Hnefatafl, Shogi), the goal piece is also a piece you protect by *avoiding capture*. Here the Lover cannot be captured at all. The contest is not "kill her before she gets there" — it is "**block her, deny her companions, deny her the Heart, force her into Silence.**" Different shape of game, different shape of thinking.

---

## A short strategic guide

- **Your Lover is your only crown-piece.** Move her every turn if it's safe. She is slow (1 cell, only toward Heart), so the march matters.
- **The Lotus is the war.** Your final escorts must be in those 8 cells when she arrives. Plan two escorts you can deliver — and disrupt theirs.
- **Block, don't bleed.** Enemy pieces in *your* Lotus prevent your Embrace. Capturing them is good, but blocking them is sometimes faster.
- **Watch the d-column.** It is the natural path for both Lovers. Whoever gets there first usually wins.
- **The Dreamer can race** to the Lotus quickly (3 cells, any direction). Sacrifice nothing — gain a free escort.
- **Don't run out of moves.** Both Soldiers and your Lover are slow. If you've parked everyone, the Silence can take you.

---

## Glossary

- **The Heart** — cell (4, 4). The centre.
- **The Lotus** — the 8 cells around the Heart. Where your escorts gather.
- **The Embrace** — the main win: Lover on Heart + ≥ 2 escorts in the Lotus.
- **The Siege** — the secondary win: opponent has no legal moves (almost always means their army is dead and their Lover is trapped alone).
- **Sacred** — the Lover. Cannot be captured.
