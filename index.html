PYAAR

The Beloved at the centre, surrounded by friends. The Lover cannot be captured.

The board

A 7 × 7 grid. The centre cell is The Heart. The 8 cells around it form the Lotus — where the Embrace forms.

Setup — before the first move

Each player arranges their seven combat pieces along their home row in any order. Click two of your back-row pieces to swap them. Shuffle Mine randomises. Press Begin Game when ready. The Soldier always starts at column d. In vs Computer, the AI's row is shuffled automatically.

Your turn

  1. Click one of your characters.
  2. Click a glowing green cell, or a red enemy to capture it.

Each piece has exactly one movement rule. The Lover cannot be captured by enemies — they may block her, trap her, or fight around her, but they may never remove her.

The eight pieces (both sides have them)

  • ♥ Lover — 1 cell toward the Heart. Cannot be captured. Your crown-piece.
  • ⚔ Warrior — 1 cell in any of 8 directions. (king)
  • ✦ Sage — any cells diagonally, stops at first piece. (bishop)
  • ⚯ Traitor — any cells orthogonally, stops at first piece. (rook)
  • ◊ Fool — L-shape jumps. (knight)
  • ⚜ Guardian — up to 2 cells in any of 8 directions.
  • ☾ Dreamer — up to 3 cells in a line. Cannot capture.
  • ⚑ Soldier — 1 cell forward, captures diagonally. (pawn)

How to win — two paths

❀ The Embrace. At the start of your turn, your Lover stands on the Heart AND at least 2 of your other pieces stand on the surrounding Lotus cells.

⚔ The Siege. At the start of your opponent's turn, they have no legal move. By the nature of PYAAR this almost always means their army is gone and their Lover is alone.

If neither fires by move 100, the army closer to the Heart wins on a tiebreaker.
