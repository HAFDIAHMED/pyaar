Eight figures, sculpted in browser-side WebGL. Each one rotates slowly so you can see every angle. ● the saffron Player 1 stands on the left; ● the peacock Player 2 stands on the right. Drag a stage to spin it by hand; release to resume the auto-spin.

Drag-to-rotate. Auto-spin paused on hover. All meshes built procedurally in Three.js — no external models.
