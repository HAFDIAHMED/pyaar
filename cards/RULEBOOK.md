# PYAAR — HIDDEN HEARTS
### *The card game of secret crushes and protected love.*

> Everyone at this table is secretly in love with someone else at this table.
> Protect your own heart. Aim it at the one you fancy. Pray they aimed back.

**Players:** 3–7 (shines at 5–7) · **Ages:** 12+ · **Time:** 15–25 min · **Type:** social bluff / light card-battle / deduction

PYAAR Cards is the card cousin of PYAAR. In the original, you escort a Lover who *cannot be captured*. Here, **every player has a Lover — their own Heart — and the game is about protecting it while secretly courting someone real.** The cards are a costume for flirting: every Crush, Heartbreak, and Friendzone you play carries plausible deniability ("relax, it's just the card!"), and at the end the hearts are revealed.

---

## 1. The one-minute version (read this first)

1. You get a **Heart** (your love). You secretly **aim it at another player** — that's your Crush. Nobody knows who you picked.
2. You secretly pick **one Character** to be your Heart's **Protector**.
3. On your turn: **draw 1 card, play 1 card.** Use Characters to attack other hearts, defend your own, peek at secrets, and send romantic signals.
4. The game ends when the deck runs out. Everyone **reveals** their Heart and their secret Crush.
5. **You win by protecting your Heart — and the jackpot is a *mutual* crush.** Most points wins.

That's the whole game. Everything below is detail.

---

## 2. The fantasy

A royal court of admirers. Each of you guards a single Heart and carries a secret: the one person across the table you've chosen. You can court them in the open with a Crush card, sting them with a playful Heartbreak, deflect an awkward advance with a Friendzone — all while never *quite* admitting anything. The clever player reads the signals, protects their love, and lands the one reveal that matters: **Soulmates.**

## 3. Components (print-and-play)

For a full 7-player set. Use fewer of each for smaller groups.

| Component | Count | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Heart cards** | 7 | Your Love. Two-sided: **Whole** / **Broken** (with a middle **Cracked** state — see §6). One per player. |
| **Seat symbols** | 7 | Identity tokens — 🌹 Rose · 🪷 Lotus · 🌙 Moon · 🔥 Flame · 🦚 Peacock · 🌼 Jasmine · ⭐ Star. Players are referred to by symbol. |
| **Character sets** | 7 sets × 7 | Each player gets their own little set of the 7 PYAAR characters to **secretly choose one Protector** (the rest go back in the box). |
| **Crush "Aim" cards** | 7 sets × 6 | Each player gets the 6 *other* seat symbols; you place one face-down = your secret Crush. (Minimalist alternative: write the symbol on a slip.) |
| **Play Deck** | 70 | The main draw-and-play deck. Full breakdown in [CARDS.md](CARDS.md). |

The **70-card Play Deck** contains: ⚔ Warrior ×9 · ✦ Sage ×7 · ⚯ Traitor ×7 · ◊ Fool ×6 · ☾ Dreamer ×6 · ⚑ Soldier ×8 · 💔 Heartbreak ×9 · 💘 Crush ×7 · 🤝 Friendzone ×8 · 💍 Devotion ×3.

> **Minimalist print:** skip the Aim cards and Character sets — just give everyone a Heart and a scrap of paper to secretly write their **Crush symbol** and their **Protector character**. The 70-card Play Deck is the only deck you truly need to print on cardstock.

## 4. Setup

1. **Seats.** Each player takes a **Heart** (Whole side up, in front of them) and a **Seat symbol**. Sit in a circle.
2. **Choose your Protector — secretly.** Take your set of 7 Characters. Secretly pick **one** and tuck it **face-down under your Heart**. This is the "chosen character" who guards your love. Return the other 6 to the box. (Each character grants a different perk — see §7.)
3. **Aim your Heart — secretly.** Look around the table. Choose the player you secretly fancy. Place that player's symbol **face-down** beside your Heart — your **Crush**. *You must pick someone; you may never pick yourself.*
4. **Deal.** Shuffle the Play Deck. Deal **5 cards** to each player. Place the rest as a face-down **draw pile**; flip the top card to start the **discard pile**.
5. Youngest player goes first. Play passes **clockwise**.

> **Keep it kind (house rule, always on).** This game points real feelings at real people. The golden rule: **a Crush is a wink, never a demand.** Anyone may "pass" a reveal by flipping their Crush to a neutral 💛 instead of a symbol. No teasing someone for their reveal. The game is funniest when everyone feels safe to play.

---

## 5. Your turn

On your turn, in order:

1. **Draw 1** card from the draw pile.
2. **Play 1** card from your hand (or choose to play none). Resolve its effect.
3. **Discard down to a hand limit of 7** if you're over.
4. Pass clockwise.

Some cards are **Reactions** (⚔ Warrior, 🤝 Friendzone) — you may play these *out of turn*, the instant something targets you. Reactions don't use up your turn.

If the **draw pile empties**, shuffle the discard pile to refresh it **once**. When it would empty a **second** time, the current round finishes and the game goes to **Reveal** (§8).

---

## 6. Your Heart: Whole → Cracked → Broken

Your Heart has three states. Attacks push it down; love pulls it back up.

- **Whole** — perfect. (Start here.)
- **Cracked** — one hit landed. (Flip to the Cracked corner / half-shade.)
- **Broken** — two hits landed. You're **Heartbroken**: you keep playing and signalling, but you score the Broken penalty and **cannot win the round outright** (you can still be a kingmaker and still reveal a mutual crush for fun).

**Protectors** sit between attacks and your Heart. A Protector is a ⚔ Warrior or ⚜ Guardian card you've played onto your own Heart (stack them). An attack must **remove a Protector first**; only a hit on a **bare** Heart cracks it.

💍 **Devotion** repairs your Heart one full step (Broken→Cracked→Whole).

---

## 7. The Characters (your Protector perks)

Whichever Character you chose as Protector in setup grants a one-time or passive perk. (It's face-down, so opponents must *deduce or peek* what's guarding you.)

| Character | Protector perk |
|---|---|
| ⚔ **Warrior** | **Bodyguard.** Cancel the first Heartbreak aimed at you for free, even without a card in hand. |
| ✦ **Sage** | **Insight.** At setup, peek at one other player's Crush. |
| ⚯ **Traitor** | **Spite.** If your Heart ever Breaks, drag one attacker down with you — remove one Protector from a Heart of your choice. |
| ◊ **Fool** | **Slippery.** You are immune to the first Friendzone played on you. |
| ⚜ **Guardian** | **Fortress.** Your Heart needs **3** hits to Break instead of 2. The sturdiest love. |
| ☾ **Dreamer** | **Vanish.** Once per game, declare yourself Untouchable until your next turn (no card can target you). |
| ⚑ **Soldier** | **Vanguard.** Draw an extra card on each of your first **3** turns. Early tempo. |

> Choosing your Protector is a real decision: Guardian is safest, Sage feeds the deduction game, Soldier rushes, Traitor punishes bullies. There is no single best pick — it depends on your table.

## 8. The cards of the Play Deck

Each card is a single, clear effect. (Exact printed text is in [CARDS.md](CARDS.md).)

**Attacks**
- 💔 **Heartbreak** — Target a player. Remove one of their Protectors; if their Heart is **bare**, **crack** it instead. *The main weapon — and a deniable "you broke my heart" jab.*
- ⚑ **Soldier** — Target a player. Remove one Protector. Cannot crack a bare Heart on its own — pure pressure.

**Defenses (Reactions — playable out of turn)**
- ⚔ **Warrior** — Cancel one attack (Heartbreak or Soldier) aimed at your Heart.
- 🤝 **Friendzone** — Cancel a **Crush or Heartbreak** aimed at you. *"Let's just be friends."* If you Friendzone a Crush, that player **cannot be your mutual** this game (the pairing is voided).
- ☾ **Dreamer** — Become **Untouchable** until your next turn. Dreamer can never attack.

**Information**
- ✦ **Sage** — Secretly look at one face-down card: any player's **Crush**, or any Heart's **Protector**. Knowledge for the endgame.

**Disruption**
- ⚯ **Traitor** — Steal one random card from a player's hand, **or** redirect an attack currently aimed at you onto someone else.
- ◊ **Fool** — **Wild:** as you play it, it copies any one Character's effect. *Or* force two players to swap one random card. Chaos.

**Love & signals (the heart of the game)**
- 💘 **Crush** — Place face-up in front of any player: a **public, deniable flirt**. If that player turns out to be your **secret Crush** and the feeling is mutual, this card scores a bonus at Reveal. You may have several Crush cards out — sincere or as cover.
- 💍 **Devotion** *(rare, ×3)* — **True love.** Either **repair your Heart one step**, *or* **declare your secret Crush now**: if they reveal the same toward you, you become **Soulmates instantly** and the round heads to Reveal. High risk, high reward.

## 9. The Reveal & scoring (the climax)

When the deck runs out the second time (or a Devotion declaration confirms a Soulmate pair), play stops. Going clockwise, **everyone flips their Heart and their secret Crush face-up at the same time.** This is the moment the whole game is built toward — savor it.

Then score:

| Outcome | Points |
|---|---|
| Your Heart is **Whole** at Reveal | **+3** |
| Your Heart is **Cracked** | **+1** |
| Your Heart is **Broken** | **−2** |
| **Mutual Crush** — you and your target secretly aimed at *each other* | **+5 each** (you are **Soulmates**) |
| You played a **Crush card** on your true secret target *and* it's mutual | **+2** (you were bold) |
| **Secret Admirer guess** — at Reveal, name one player you think crushed on you. Right: **+2**. Wrong: 0. | +2 / 0 |
| Your Crush was **Friendzoned** by its target | pairing voided, mutual impossible |

**The winner** is the player with the **most points** — *"the one who protected their love."*

**Ties** break in this order: (1) most-intact Heart, (2) being half of a Soulmate pair, (3) the most Crush cards received from others — the table's sweetheart.

> **Design note — two ways to feel like you won.** Mechanically, the high scorer wins. *Emotionally,* a Soulmate pair always "wins the night" no matter the points. Both are real; both are celebrated.

## 10. Variants

- **Quick Love (10 min).** Deal 4 cards, use 40 of the 70 Play cards, first player to land any mutual crush ends the game immediately.
- **Cupid (8+ players / odd one out).** One extra player is **Cupid** — no Heart. Cupid draws/plays normally but only Signal and Info cards, trying to *engineer* mutual crushes between others. Cupid scores +2 for every Soulmate pair they helped create.
- **Tournament.** Play 3 rounds, re-seat between rounds, sum the scores. The romance resets each round — new secret crushes every time.
- **Open Hearts (lighter, less bluff).** Crushes are placed face-up from the start. Pure protect-and-signal; great for kids/large casual groups.

---

## 11. Strategy in three lines

- **Protect quietly, court loudly.** Stack a Protector or two early; spend Crush cards to fish for a mutual without exposing your real target.
- **Heartbreak is also a flirt.** Aiming a Heartbreak at your secret crush is the oldest move in the book — deniable, and it keeps your real target guessing.
- **Watch the Friendzones.** A Friendzone tells you exactly who *doesn't* want a pairing — which narrows down who *does*.

---

## 12. Glossary

- **Heart** — your Love; the card you protect. Whole → Cracked → Broken.
- **Crush** — your secret aim at another player (a real person at the table).
- **Protector** — the Character you chose to guard your Heart.
- **Mutual / Soulmates** — two players who secretly aimed at each other. The jackpot.
- **Friendzoned** — a Crush deflected by its target; that pairing can never be mutual.
- **Untouchable** — cannot be targeted by any card until your next turn.

## 13. Digital edition — differences from the tabletop rules

There is a free **pass-and-play web version** ([web/index.html](web/index.html)) — one device, with AI opponents. It follows these rules with two deliberate adaptations, tuned across thousands of simulated games to a tight ~15-minute match:

1. **The deck makes a single pass (no reshuffle).** The round ends the moment the draw pile can no longer feed a turn — a natural clock. (The tabletop game reshuffles once for a longer session.) A game also ends early if **only one Heart is still unbroken** ("last heart standing").
2. **Defense is proactive, not reactive.** A single shared screen can't hide out-of-turn reactions, so on the app you defend *on your own turn*: a ⚔ **Warrior** is played onto your own Heart as a 🛡 Protector, and 🤝 **Friendzone** is played actively. The Warrior **Protector-perk** (Bodyguard) still auto-cancels the first Heartbreak aimed at you, so the defensive flavour survives.

Everything else — secret crushes, the Protector characters and their perks, all ten cards, the Reveal, and scoring — matches this rulebook. The printed game remains the definitive, fullest version; the app is the quick, solo-friendly way to play.

> **Balance, for the curious.** With these settings ~56% of hearts survive the night, a Soulmate pair forms in roughly half of all games, and the winner almost always finishes with an intact Heart — so "protect your love" is a goal you can actually reach.
