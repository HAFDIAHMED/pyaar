# PYAAR — Build Your Love
### *The card game of secret crushes.*

> Everyone at the table secretly fancies someone. Build your romance, work up the
> courage to **commit** — and pray they feel the same. Mutual love wins. 💞

**Players:** 3–6 (best at 3–5) · **Ages:** 14+ · **Time:** ~15 min · **Type:** social / flirting / light deduction

---

## 1. The one-minute version
1. You secretly **fancy one other player** (your crush). Nobody knows who.
2. On your turn: **draw 1 card, play 1.**
3. Play ❤️ **Moment** cards to grow your romance up three stages: **✨ Spark → 🌹 Dating → 💋 Crazy-for-them.**
4. Once you reach 💋, you may **Commit** (play another ❤️) — *declare your crush out loud.* If they secretly fancy **you** back → **you both win as Soulmates.** If not → you're **rejected** (your secret's out, you slip back a stage).
5. Use 👀 **Glance** to peek at who someone fancies (find out if your crush likes you *before* you risk it), 💔 **Heartbreak** to knock a rival back, and more.

First mutual commit wins. That's the whole game.

---

## 2. The fantasy
This is a flirting game in disguise. Your "crush" is a real person at the table, and every Glance, every Sway, every Commit is a deniable signal — *"it's just the card!"* The magic is the will-they-won't-they, the secret rivalry (two people can fancy the same person), and the held-breath moment when someone finally commits and the table learns who loved whom.

---

## 3. Components (print-and-play)
- **52-card Play Deck** — see [CARDS.md](CARDS.md) for the printable manifest.
- **7 Seat tokens** — 🌹 Rose · 🪷 Lotus · 🌙 Moon · 🔥 Flame · 🦚 Peacock · 🌼 Jasmine · ⭐ Star. Players are known by symbol.
- **A Love Line track per player** — a small strip showing ✨ 🌹 💋, plus a marker (a coin works) to show your current stage.
- **A way to keep your crush secret** — a set of seat-symbol "Aim" slips, or just write it down. Set it once at the start; it only changes if you play 💘 Sway.

---

## 4. Setup
1. Each player takes a **Seat token** and a **Love Line track** (marker at the start, before ✨).
2. **Secretly choose your crush** — another player. Lock it face-down. *You must pick someone; never yourself.*
3. Shuffle the Play Deck; deal **3 cards** to each player. Place the rest as a draw pile.
4. Youngest player goes first (or random). Play passes clockwise.

> **🕊️ Keep it kind (always on).** This game points real feelings at real people. House rules that make it safe — and *more* fun: (a) A crush is a wink, never a demand. (b) Anyone may decline a reveal — flip a neutral 💛 instead of your real crush. (c) A **quiet commit**: if you'd rather not risk public rejection, you and your crush may resolve a commit *privately* — only the two of you see the result; the table just hears "match" or "no match." (d) No teasing anyone for their reveal. The game is best when everyone feels safe enough to be bold.

---

## 5. Your turn — draw 1, play 1
On your turn: **draw 1 card**, then **play 1 card** and resolve it. If you truly can't or won't use a card, discard one (you must play or discard something). Then pass.

### The six cards
| Card | What it does |
|---|---|
| ❤️ **Moment** | Advance your romance one stage (✨→🌹→💋). **At 💋, playing a Moment is your Commit** (see below). |
| 👀 **Glance** | Secretly look at **who one player fancies.** Your best tool — peek at your crush to learn if they like you back *before* you commit. |
| 💘 **Sway** | Re-aim **your** crush at a new player. Your romance **cools one stage** (your eye wandered). Use it to chase someone who actually likes you. |
| 💔 **Heartbreak** | Knock a rival **back one stage.** (A player can only be knocked back **once per round** — they're then numb until their next turn.) |
| 🛡️ **Guardian** | **Shield** yourself — blocks the next Heartbreak or Friendzone aimed at you. |
| 🤝 **Friendzone** | A rival **loses their next turn.** |

---

## 6. The Commit — the moment of truth
When your Love Line is at **💋 Crazy-for-them**, you may **Commit** (play a ❤️ Moment): you declare aloud who your crush is.
- If that player **secretly fancies you too → you both WIN as Soulmates 💞.** Game over.
- If they don't → you're **rejected**: reveal your crush to everyone, slip back to 🌹, and lose your next turn (nursing a broken heart).

This is the heart of the game. **Don't commit blind** — 👀 Glance your crush first to check, or read the table. Or take the leap and gamble on love.

---

## 7. How to win
- **Soulmates** — the first player to a **mutual commit** wins (with their crush). The dream.
- **Closest to love** — if the deck runs out before anyone seals it, the player **furthest along** their Love Line wins the night (ties broken in favour of a mutual pair).

---

## 8. A short strategy guide
- **Scout before you leap.** A Glance on your crush turns a terrifying gamble into a sure thing. Hold your Commit until you know — or are willing to risk it.
- **Chase who chases you.** If your crush doesn't fancy you back, Sway toward someone who does (you'll find out by Glancing around). It costs a stage, but unrequited love never wins.
- **Snipe the frontrunner.** Heartbreak the player who looks ready to commit — knock them off 💋 right before they seal it.
- **Guard at the brink.** When you're at 💋 and about to commit, a Guardian protects you from a last-second Heartbreak.

---

## 9. Glossary
- **Crush** — the player you secretly fancy (changeable only via Sway).
- **Love Line** — your romance track: ✨ Spark → 🌹 Dating → 💋 Crazy-for-them.
- **Commit** — declaring your crush at 💋; wins only if it's mutual.
- **Soulmates** — two players who secretly fancy each other. The win.
