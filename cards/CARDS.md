# PYAAR — Hidden Hearts · Card List (print-and-play manifest)

Everything you need to print the deck. Print on cardstock, cut, and sleeve if you can.
Suggested card size: **63 × 88 mm** (standard poker / "bridge" size).

Color code each card by family so the table reads fast:
**Attack = red · Defense = blue · Info = green · Disruption = purple · Love = pink · Devotion = gold.**

---

## A. THE PLAY DECK — 70 cards

> Title · count · family · the exact text to print on the card.

### Attacks (red)

**💔 Heartbreak ×9**
> *"Break a heart."* Target one player. Remove one of their Protectors. If their Heart is **bare**, **crack** it instead (Whole→Cracked→Broken). Can be cancelled by Warrior or Friendzone.

**⚑ Soldier ×8**
> *"Advance."* Target one player. Remove one of their Protectors. Cannot crack a bare Heart by itself — pure pressure.

### Defenses (blue) — *Reactions: play out of turn*

**⚔ Warrior ×9**
> *"Shield."* **Reaction.** Cancel one attack (Heartbreak or Soldier) aimed at your Heart. May also be played onto your own Heart as a Protector.

**🤝 Friendzone ×8**
> *"Let's just be friends."* **Reaction.** Cancel a Crush **or** Heartbreak aimed at you. If you cancel a Crush, that player can never be your mutual this game.

**☾ Dreamer ×6**
> *"Daydream."* Until your next turn you are **Untouchable** — no card may target you. The Dreamer can never attack.

### Information (green)

**✦ Sage ×7**
> *"Insight."* Secretly look at one face-down card: any player's **Crush**, or any Heart's **Protector**. Tell no one (or lie).

### Disruption (purple)

**⚯ Traitor ×7**
> *"Betrayal."* Steal one random card from a player's hand, **OR** redirect an attack now aimed at you onto a different player.

**◊ Fool ×6**
> *"Wild."* As you play this, it copies any **one** Character's effect of your choice. **OR** force two players to swap one random card each.

### Love & signals (pink / gold)

**💘 Crush ×7** *(pink)*
> *"Send a sign."* Place face-up in front of any player — a public, deniable flirt. If that player is your **secret Crush** and it ends mutual, this card scores **+2** at Reveal. Keep played Crush cards in front of their targets.

**💍 Devotion ×3** *(gold, rare)*
> *"True love."* Either **repair your Heart one step**, **OR** declare your secret Crush aloud now — if they secretly aimed at you too, you become **Soulmates** and the round goes to Reveal.

**Play Deck total:** 9+8+9+8+6+7+7+6+7+3 = **70 cards.**

## B. PER-PLAYER COMPONENTS (not shuffled into the Play Deck)

### Hearts — 7 cards (one per player)
Two-sided / three-state. Front shows a glowing whole heart; design a **Cracked** middle state (a single fracture) and a **Broken** back (split in two). Big and beautiful — this is the card everyone reveals at the end.
> Print 7. Optionally tint each to match a seat symbol.

### Seat symbols — 7 tokens
🌹 Rose · 🪷 Lotus · 🌙 Moon · 🔥 Flame · 🦚 Peacock · 🌼 Jasmine · ⭐ Star.
> One per player, placed in front of them so everyone is named by symbol.

### Character sets — 7 sets of 7 (49 small cards)
Each player gets one full set of the 7 PYAAR characters to **secretly choose a Protector** from, then returns the unused 6. Each card prints its perk (from Rulebook §7):

| Character | Perk text to print |
|---|---|
| ⚔ Warrior | **Bodyguard** — cancel the first Heartbreak aimed at you for free. |
| ✦ Sage | **Insight** — at setup, peek at one other player's Crush. |
| ⚯ Traitor | **Spite** — if your Heart Breaks, remove one Protector from any Heart. |
| ◊ Fool | **Slippery** — immune to the first Friendzone played on you. |
| ⚜ Guardian | **Fortress** — your Heart needs 3 hits to Break instead of 2. |
| ☾ Dreamer | **Vanish** — once per game, be Untouchable until your next turn. |
| ⚑ Soldier | **Vanguard** — draw an extra card on each of your first 3 turns. |

### Crush "Aim" cards — 7 sets of 6 (42 small cards)
Each player gets the 6 seat symbols that **aren't their own**; they place one face-down at setup = their secret Crush. Plus a neutral **💛 "Pass"** card per player for the keep-it-kind rule.
> *Minimalist alternative:* skip all the Character + Aim cards (91 small cards) and just hand each player a slip of paper to secretly write their **Protector** and **Crush symbol**. Only the 70-card Play Deck must be printed.

## E. PRINTING TIPS

- 9 cards per A4/Letter sheet at poker size → the Play Deck is ~8 sheets.
- Print fronts; use a single shared **card back** per family (or one PYAAR back for all) so hands stay hidden.
- Round the corners, sleeve in opaque sleeves so the Hearts and Crushes can't be read through the back.
