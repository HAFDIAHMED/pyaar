# PYAAR — Build Your Love · Card List (print-and-play manifest)

Print on cardstock, cut, sleeve if you can. Suggested size **63 × 88 mm** (poker).
Color-code by family: **love = pink · attack = red · info = green · defense = blue · jealousy = teal.**

---

## A. THE PLAY DECK — 52 cards

> Title · count · the exact text to print.

**❤️ Moment ×21** *(love)*
> *"Grow closer."* Advance your romance one stage: ✨ Spark → 🌹 Dating → 💋 Crazy-for-them. **At 💋, playing a Moment is your Commit** — declare your crush; if they fancy you back you both win, if not you're rejected (back to 🌹, lose a turn).

**👀 Glance ×8** *(info)*
> *"Scout a heart."* Secretly look at **who one player fancies.** Check if your crush likes you *before* you commit.

**💘 Sway ×5** *(love)*
> *"Fall for another."* Re-aim **your** crush at a new player. Your romance **cools one stage.**

**💔 Heartbreak ×5** *(attack)*
> *"Break a heart."* Knock **any** rival **back one stage.** A player can only be knocked back once per round.

**💚 Jealousy ×4** *(jealousy)*
> *"Green with envy."* Choose a rival who is **🌹 Dating or closer**: knock them **back one stage AND turn their secret crush face-up for everyone to see.** (If they were already knocked back this round, the setback is absorbed but you still expose them.) Blocked by Guardian.

**🛡️ Guardian ×4** *(defense)*
> *"Guard your love."* Block the next **Heartbreak, Jealousy or Friendzone** aimed at you.

**🤝 Friendzone ×5** *(defense)*
> *"Just friends."* A rival **loses their next turn.**

**Play Deck total:** 21 + 8 + 5 + 5 + 4 + 4 + 5 = **52 cards.**

## B. PER-PLAYER COMPONENTS

### Love Line tracks — one per player (up to 7)
A small strip printed with three slots: **✨ Spark · 🌹 Dating · 💋 Crazy-for-them** (and a 💍 "Commit!" marker past 💋). Each player puts a token/coin on their current stage and slides it as they grow.

### Seat tokens — 7
🌹 Rose · 🪷 Lotus · 🌙 Moon · 🔥 Flame · 🦚 Peacock · 🌼 Jasmine · ⭐ Star — one per player.

### Secret "Crush" slips — 7 sets of 6 (+ a 💛 Pass)
Each player gets the 6 seat-symbols that **aren't their own**; place one face-down = your secret crush. Plus a neutral **💛** for the keep-it-kind opt-out.
> *Minimalist:* skip these and just **write your crush's symbol** on a scrap, face-down. Only the 52-card Play Deck must be printed on cardstock.

## D. SCALING BY PLAYER COUNT
| Players | Notes |
|---|---|
| 3–5 | The sweet spot — mutual commits land often, ~15 min. |
| 6 | Plays, but more games end on "closest to love." |
| 7+ | Not recommended (the leader gets ganged up on before sealing). |

## E. PRINTING TIPS
- 9 cards per A4/Letter at poker size → the 52-card deck is ~6 sheets.
- One shared **card back** keeps hands hidden; opaque sleeves stop see-through.
- Want ready-to-print sheets? Open **[print-sheet.html](print-sheet.html)** — it lays the deck out 9-up and exports PNG/PDF.
